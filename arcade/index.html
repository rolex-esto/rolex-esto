How to play
  • WASD / ←↑↓→ move · P, Esc or Space pause · M mute
  • Collect all tokens to open the portal ▣. Checkpoint flags ⚑ save your spot.
  • Power-ups: ♥ patch (+1 HP), ◈ firewall shield, ⚡ cache boost (speed).
  • Two continues per run. Assist mode: slower bugs, more HP, lighter darkness.
  • Touch: turn on the touch pad (it turns on automatically on touch screens).
